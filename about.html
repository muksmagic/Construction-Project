OUR STORY

Thirty years of building quietly across the Cape Peninsula.

LD Projects was founded in 1994 on a simple premise: that a small, senior team should build the buildings — not manage layers of subcontractors. That decision still shapes how we work today. Each commission is led personally by one of our directors, from first conversation to handover.

Over the years we have delivered more than one hundred projects across the Atlantic Seaboard, the City Bowl, the Southern Suburbs and the Winelands — homes, offices, retail and mixed-use developments — each one answered with the same patient craft.
